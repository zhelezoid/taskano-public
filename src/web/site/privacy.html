Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Taskano is a task tool: AI agents assign work to people, and people do it through a Telegram bot and a Mini App. This page describes exactly what the service stores, why, where it is kept and how to have it removed. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who is responsible

The service is operated by the Taskano team, reachable at support@taskano.app. Questions about data, including deletion requests, go to the same address; see Support.

About the work

Tasks and everything attached to them: title, the reason behind the task, what counts as done, due dates, status, notes people write, questions they ask, reasons they decline, time spent, links and files added to a task, and photos forwarded from work chats. Agents connected to an account also store their plans, decisions and proposals.

Technical records

An event log of what happened to each task (who took it, when it was submitted, when it was accepted) — this is how the product works, not analytics. Server logs contain request errors and timestamps. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this site.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not train AI models on your data.
  • We do not read Telegram chats. The bot receives only messages addressed to it, and in work groups only what is explicitly forwarded to it.
  • We do not request access to your Claude conversation history, memory or files.

Where the data is kept

The database and the application run on servers in Germany. Data leaves them only towards the providers listed above, and only the part each of them needs.

The Claude connector

When you connect Taskano to Claude, the connector acts within one account and only through the tools the connector declares. Access is granted by OAuth: you approve it, and you can revoke it at any time in Claude's connector settings — after that the tokens stop working. The connector does not read your conversations; it receives only what a tool call passes to it.

How long we keep it

  • Work data — while the account exists.
  • Sign-in codes — minutes; they are single use.
  • Server logs — up to 30 days.
  • Database backups — up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.

Deleting data

A person removed from a company stops seeing its work immediately. To have an account and its personal data deleted, write to support@taskano.app from the address on the account; we do this within 30 days. Tasks that belong to a company remain with the company, with the person's name replaced — otherwise the company would lose its own history of work it paid for. Backups are overwritten on the schedule above.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or receive a copy. Write to support@taskano.app. If you are in the EU or the UK, these rights are the ones granted to you by the GDPR.

Children

The service is for work and is not intended for people under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or whom we share it with, the date at the top changes and account owners are notified by email before the change takes effect.

Security. Data of different companies is separated in the database itself, not only in application code: a request made on behalf of one company cannot read another company's rows. Traffic runs over TLS. Secrets and tokens are stored encrypted.
