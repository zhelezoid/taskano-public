Documentation

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Taskano connects Claude to the people who do the work. You set tasks, follow up and accept results in a conversation with Claude; each person receives one clear task at a time in Telegram, in their own language, and answers there.

Who it is for

  • People who hand out work — a company owner, a manager, or Claude agents acting for them. They work in Claude through the Taskano connector.
  • People who do the work — they use the Telegram bot @Taskano_bot and the small app inside Telegram (Mini App). They need no Claude account. Tasks, reminders and answers are translated for each of them; the bot itself speaks English, Russian, Turkish and Vietnamese, and for other languages it shows an English interface while the text of the tasks is still translated.

What you need

  • A Claude account on a plan that lets you add connectors — either Taskano from the Claude connector directory or a custom connector by URL.
  • A Google account or any email address. Sign in with Google, or with a one-time code sent to your email; there are no passwords.
  • A Telegram account for every person who will receive tasks.
Price. Taskano is free during early access. Paid plans will come; existing accounts will be told in advance, and nothing is charged automatically — we do not ask for a card. See Terms.

Setup

1. Connect Taskano to Claude

  1. Add Taskano from the connector directory in Claude, or add a custom connector: in claude.ai open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, name it Taskano and use the URL https://taskano.app/mcp.
  2. Press Connect. A Taskano page opens: press Sign in with Google, or enter your email and then the code from the letter we send.
  3. Press Allow. Claude can now act in Taskano on your behalf — create tasks, read their status and reports. You can revoke this at any time in Claude's connector settings.

The connector address and other clients

Taskano is a remote MCP server at https://taskano.app/mcp (Streamable HTTP, OAuth 2.1 sign-in with dynamic client registration).

  • Claude on the web, desktop and mobile — a custom connector added in claude.ai is available in all of them.
  • Claude Code — claude mcp add --transport http taskano https://taskano.app/mcp, then /mcp in Claude Code to sign in.
  • Other MCP clients — any client that supports remote servers with OAuth connects with the same address.

2. Add the Taskano skill (recommended)

The connector works on its own, but the skill teaches Claude how to lead people well: how to phrase a task so it can be checked, whom to give it to, when to follow up. It is open source at github.com/example-org/taskano-skill. In claude.ai open Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload skill and upload the skills/taskano folder from that repository as a ZIP archive. In Claude Code, copy the same folder to ~/.claude/skills/taskano. The repository README has the details.

3. Create your company

Tell Claude: “Set up my company in Taskano.” Claude creates it in the conversation — only the company name is needed — and asks the rest as it comes up. A new company starts in English and UTC; say your language and city (for example “we work in Berlin time”) and Claude sets the language and time zone. Settings can be changed in any later conversation.

4. Invite people

Name each person and their email: “Invite Anna, anna@example.com, she speaks Turkish and runs the warehouse.” Anna receives an email with a link to the Telegram bot. She opens it, agrees to the terms, confirms her time zone, and from then on receives tasks in the bot. Until a person has signed in, they receive nothing — Claude can tell you who has.

Link your own Telegram too: open @Taskano_bot, send /start and sign in with the same email. Without this Taskano has no way to reach you with decisions, proposals and alerts.

5. Give the first task

“Have Anna photograph shelf B4 and count item 4471 by 5 pm today — the marketplace shows 12, the warehouse sheet says 3.” Claude records what to do, why, what counts as done and the due date, and the task lands in Anna's queue in Telegram, in Turkish.

Everyday use

You speak to Claude in your own words; Claude chooses the tools. Some examples:

You sayWhat happens
“What's waiting for me in Taskano?”A briefing: what changed, results waiting for review, blocked work and open questions, people left without a next step, decided proposals.
“Have Mark send the samples to the client by Friday.”A task for Mark with the reason, the done criteria and the date. He gets it in Telegram.
“Ask Mark to name the date for the price list himself.”Mark is asked to set the due date and picks it in a calendar in the app. No answer in a working day — he gets a reminder; in two — you hear about it.
“Tell Anna to count only sealed boxes.”The answer goes to Anna's open question, translated, and the task is unblocked.
“Mark's price list looks right, accept it.” / “Send it back — the VAT column is missing.”The result is accepted, or returned for rework with your comment.
“How is Anna doing?”What she is doing now, her queue, what she is waiting on, her open questions and what changed since the last look.
“How many hours did the team log last week?”Hours by day, by task or by area, with days counted in each person's own time zone.
“Make a closed project ‘Q4 hiring’ for me and Maria.”A project only its members see; tasks can be filed under it.
“I've sent the contract, that one is done.”A task you set for yourself is closed as done at once — there is nobody else to accept it — and counts in the results.
“Remind me to call the accountant on Monday.”An item in your personal space, not visible to the company.

Claude agents can also lead an area for you — own a plan, break it into steps, assign them and check the results. Anything that matters (money, new people, closing a task someone else set) comes to you as a proposal to accept or decline.

Work chats

Add @Taskano_bot to a team chat in Telegram and make it an administrator. It stays silent, files photos and messages from members of your company under the right tasks, and proposes tasks from clear assignments. Messages from people who have not signed in to Taskano are not stored.

Working while you are away

Without extra setup, agents catch up each time you open a conversation with Claude. If people start waiting for answers while you are away, you can connect a Claude routine that runs the agents on a schedule; the skill's setup guide walks through it.

AI budgetEach company has a monthly AI budget, $20 by default, which the owner can change.

The AI budget pays for translation and for reading work chats — not for your conversations with Claude. When it runs out, the owner is notified, and translation and chat analysis pause: people see text in the language it was written in. They resume on their own once the budget is raised, or when the next month starts.

Data and privacy

The database and the application run on servers in Germany (Hetzner). Taskano stores what the work needs — people's names, emails, Telegram IDs, languages and time zones, and the tasks with everything attached to them. Text is sent to Anthropic to be translated and, for linked work chats, to be filed under the right task. Data of different companies is separated in the database itself. The connector does not read your Claude conversations; it receives only what a tool call passes to it. We do not sell data and do not train models on it. The full list of what is stored, who processes it and how to have it deleted is in the Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

The sign-in code did not arrive

Look in spam, and check the address you typed. Codes are single-use and expire within minutes — if some time has passed, start the sign-in again to get a new one.

Claude says I have to name a company

You belong to more than one company. Say which one you mean — by name is enough.

An invited person gets no tasks

They have not opened the invitation link and started the bot yet. Ask Claude to resend the invitation — a new link goes to the same email. The link is never shown to you: whoever opens it signs in as that person, so it reaches only the owner of the address.

“This Telegram is already linked to someone else”

That Telegram account belongs to another Taskano user, and accounts are never merged. Sign in with the email that account already uses, or use another Telegram account.

Tasks arrive untranslated

The company's AI budget for the month has run out. Ask Claude to raise it — translation resumes within a few minutes. Otherwise it resumes on its own when the next month starts.

Creating a company or inviting someone is refused

One of the limits above has been reached. The message says which one and when it will be possible again.

The connector fails to connect

Remove it in Claude's connector settings and add it again with https://taskano.app/mcp. If it still fails, write to us with the time it happened.

Support

Write to support@taskano.app. Mention the task's short number (like NOR-014) or the company name, and whether it happened in Claude, in the bot or in the Mini App. More on the Support page.
