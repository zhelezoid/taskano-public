From one conversation to a working team

You talk to Claude. Taskano keeps the facts: people, tasks, plans, what is waiting on whom. The agents do the managing.

  1. Describe your company

    Tell Claude who works with you and what each area needs. People, agents and working hours are set up from the conversation.

  2. Agents lead each area

    Purchasing, marketplaces, the warehouse. Each agent owns a plan, breaks it into steps and assigns them.

  3. People work in Telegram

    One task at a time, with the reason and what counts as done. Questions go straight to the agent.

  4. The doer names the date

    Ask a person to set the due date themselves. They pick it in a calendar, and you see who committed to what and when. No answer in a working day, and they get a reminder; two, and you hear about it.

Simple for the people doing the work

No boards to learn and no backlog to scroll. The bot shows what to do now and why it matters.

  • In their own language

    Tasks, reminders and answers are translated for each person. English, Russian, Turkish and Vietnamese today.

  • One thing at a time

    Each task says what to do, why, and what counts as done. Photos and results go back with one tap.

  • Nothing to install

    A Telegram bot and a small app inside Telegram. Reminders follow each person's working hours.

You stay in charge

Agents handle the routine and tell you what they did. Anything that matters waits for your decision.

  • 24 hours to undo

    When an agent changes something on its own, you get a line with an Undo button.

  • Proposals for the big calls

    Money, new people, closing a task someone set: the agent proposes, you accept or decline.

  • A spending limit

    Each company has a monthly AI budget. When it runs out, translation and chat analysis pause until you raise it.

Work chats, filed automatically

Add Taskano to a team chat and make it an admin. It stays quiet, reads photos and messages from your team, and files them under the right task. When someone writes that it's done, it asks them to confirm. Messages that match no task are deleted after 30 days.

Connect from Claude and other MCP clients

Taskano is a remote MCP server. One address works everywhere, and sign-in opens in your browser: with Google, or with a one-time code from your email.

https://taskano.app/mcp

Claude on the web, desktop and mobile
Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste the address. Added once, it works in all of them.
Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http taskano https://taskano.app/mcp, then /mcp to sign in.
Other MCP clients
Streamable HTTP with OAuth 2.1 and dynamic client registration — the same address.

Start in three steps

  1. In Claude open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste https://taskano.app/mcp
  2. Sign in with Google, or with a one-time code sent to your email. No password.
  3. Tell Claude “Set up my company in Taskano” — Claude creates it and helps you set the language, the time zone and invite your people.

Open to everyone, free during early access. The skill teaches Claude to set tasks well; the connector works without it.
